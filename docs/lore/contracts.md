# Contract briefings

Twelve briefings in the voice of the person handing the job over. Each names a real raid location. `canon` is noted
per contract; all handlers and specifics are new.

---

### 1. "The Customs Cage" — Spaceport
*Handler: Rosie (trader). canon: new*

Listen, love. Customs Hall B took a crate off a friend of mine last week: radio valves, forty of them, mine
already paid for. It's in the cage behind Desk 3 with everything else they've "forfeited". I don't care what else
you carry out of there, but my valves come home. Security's contracted, so they're paid by the month and it's the
end of the month. Go loud if you like. I'll be at the stall pretending I've never met you.

### 2. "Fill the Slate" — Navy Outpost
*Handler: Wolff (trader). canon: new*

The Outpost's armoury watch is empty from 0400. I have it from a man who drinks with their signals sergeant. In
that armoury are twelve crates of service 1911s, stamped, never signed for by anybody who'll miss them. Bring me
the pistols. Leave the Marines alone where you can: a dead Marine brings a frigate, a robbed Marine brings a form.
The east approach is mined. Do not walk the east approach.

### 3. "Gallery Nine" — Iridium Asteroid Mine
*Handler: Overlord House, Clerk 9 (by letter). canon: fan-theory-based (A742)*

The House requires one Class III canister recovered from the Company refinery store adjoining Gallery 9, where the
face has been reported "warm". The canister is to be delivered **sealed**. The House accepts no liability for
canisters delivered open, nor for the persons who opened them. The miners are not party to this arrangement and
need not be inconvenienced. The fee is generous. Interest on late delivery accrues daily.

### 4. "Claim Jumpers" — Mini Mine
*Handler: B. Ferenc, former owner of Claim 2207 (radio, nervous). canon: new*

You don't know me. The Company took my claim for a debt I've paid twice. Their strongbox is in the shaft-head hut:
the claim deeds, a year's wages, and my brother's watch. One guard. He's young; give him a chance to run, he will.
Take the money, take the deeds, burn the deeds. Bring me the watch. That's all I want. Well. Most of all.

### 5. "The Ore Boat" — Mining Frigate
*Handler: First Mate Calico (guild). canon: new*

Company's recalled the escort off the Ring lane again. That leaves a fat mining frigate with a full hold and a
strongroom key on a string round somebody's neck: the foreman's, or the cook's if the master's clever. The crusher
doesn't stop, so don't fall in it, and don't try to stop it either. Last crew that took this job lost a man to the
corridor mines. Read his notes if you find him. Guild gets ten per cent. It always gets ten per cent.

### 6. "Beta Five" — Penal Colony
*Handler: the Pirate Trader. canon: official-based (Penal Colony Beta 5); job new*

Warden Mott's short eleven guards since the flu. Smelter's running two shifts, which means the payroll vault is
full and the Warden's doing his own rounds. Get in, get the payroll, and if you happen across his riot helmet I'll
pay you three hundred and a drink. Prisoners'll ask you to take them along. Your ship, your call. Just remember a
convict in your hold eats your rations.

### 7. "Rifles for Leaflets" — Rebel Base
*Handler: Major R. Ffoulkes, SAS (sealed envelope). canon: new*

We would be most grateful if the printing press at the so-called Rebel Base were to stop printing. Its broadsheets
are reaching Beta 5, which the Ministry finds untidy. You may keep whatever arms you find; I'm told they count
every round, so they'll notice. The Kingdom has no involvement in this matter and will deny having written this
letter, which you will burn.

### 8. "Absent Friends" — Capital Ship
*Handler: Greybeard (trader). canon: new*

Guest night on the flagship this Saturday. Every officer in the wardroom, in mess dress, sidearms on since the
last lot took the silver. That means the paymaster's office two decks down is minded by a steward and a lock.
Paymaster's safe, the Captain's chart case, and if you see real potatoes in the galley, bring a sack. Don't go near
the wardroom. They've laid four empty places already. Don't make it five, or nine.

### 9. "Two Manifests" — Merchant Ship
*Handler: Rosie (trader). canon: new*

The *Faithful Margaret*'s outbound with tinned fish in Hold 1. Under the fish are twenty cases of Thompson drums
Wolff thinks are his. In the strongroom there's a lead can Overlord thinks is his. Captain Petrie thinks it's all
hers. Take the drums, I'll sell them back to Wolff. Leave the can unless you're mad. Petrie will look tearful.
She's very good at it.

### 10. "Pods Three Hundred to Three-Forty" — Colony Cruiser
*Handler: First Mate Calico (guild). canon: official-based (Colony Cruiser); job new*

UA cruiser *Providence*, day two-sixty-odd of a long haul, half rations, Lab 2 gone bad. They're waking their
reserve pods on day two-seventy, which means Marines groggy and Medics busy. We want the fungus cultures from Lab 1,
sealed jars, and the purser's ration cards. Don't touch the sleepers. Don't touch the labs' equipment. A cruiser
that can't feed itself drifts, and a drifting cruiser is a graveyard with our name on it.

### 11. "The Seed Vault" — Terraformer
*Handler: a man in a good coat, cog on his cufflinks (in person, unnamed). canon: fan-theory-based (echo of 1976)*

A terraformer platform in the outer sector has a seed vault of exceptional quality. The vault is to be emptied.
The farmers are of no interest to my principals on this occasion. I stress, on this occasion. Payment in marks,
half now. You will hear stories about Proxima in your bars. You will find that nobody who tells them was there.

### 12. "Forty Turns" — Planet Killer
*Handler: Overlord House, Clerk 9 (by letter). canon: official-based (Planet Killer); A742 warheads fan-theory-based*

The House has been asked by a party it cannot name to ensure that Silo 2's launch keys go missing. Both keys. The
House has no interest in the warheads and strongly advises you share that disinterest. The launch crew sleep inside
the blast door, which will be shut. You will need a torch, patience, and no sense of irony. Fee on delivery of both
keys. One key is worth nothing; the House pays for pairs.
