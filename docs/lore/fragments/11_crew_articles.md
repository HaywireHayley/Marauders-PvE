**ARTICLES OF THE SECOND CHANCE**
*Signed or marked by every hand. Read aloud on every new moon, or when somebody forgets.*

**I.** The take is split by shares. Captain two, gunner one and a half, everyone else one. Dead men's shares go
to whoever they named, or to the pot.

**II.** No man or woman opens a lead can aboard this ship. You bring it home shut or you leave it.

**III.** No one boards ahead of the torch. The torch goes first. The torch is paid extra.

**IV.** Leave the farmers be. Farmers grow what we eat. (Their stores are fair game.)

**V.** Whoever leaves a mate in a corridor to save their own skin is put out at the next gate. Without the ship.

**VI.** Nobody wears the Baron's cap aboard. We've had enough trouble.

**VII.** The gate closes for no one. Be at the ship when the captain calls it, or be a story.

**VIII.** Quarrels are settled ashore, with fists, and the loser buys.

**IX.** What's said in the dark stays in the dark.
