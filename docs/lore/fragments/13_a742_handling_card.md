**⚠ ELEMENT A742 — HANDLING CARD — CLASS III CANISTER ⚠**
*Joint Translunar Authority pattern, 1958. Reissued by each holding power. Keep beside cradle.*

**THE CANISTER IS THE ONLY THING BETWEEN YOU AND IT.**

1. Lift with two hands and the strap. Never by the lid.
2. Never set it down on a heavier object. Never set a heavier object on it.
3. The canister will be warm. A canister that is **cold** has failed. Clear the compartment and seal it.
4. Watches, compasses and gauges near the canister will drift. This is normal. Do not navigate by them.
5. Do not shake. Do not drop. If you drop it, **do not pick it up.** Withdraw, seal the hatch, report.
6. A loose sample forms a dark sphere the size of a ball. It will grow if fed. Do not feed it. Do not try
   to retrieve it. Do not look for it with a torch.
7. Exposure: nosebleed, metal taste, fatigue. Report to the medic and stop handling for 30 days.

**Maximum handling per man per month: 4 hours.**

*Remember Dudinka.*

(scratched in the enamel with a knife:) *remember to wear gloves*
