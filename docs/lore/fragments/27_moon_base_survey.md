**SOUTH DOME — SURVEY HUT 3 — DAY BOOK**

**Mon.** Regolith works down again. Dust in the bearings. It is always dust in the bearings.

**Tue.** Walked the line to Hut 5. 2.4 miles, 1 hr 50. Suit heater failing on the left leg. The cold doesn't
hurt after the first mile, which is what worries me.

**Wed.** Supply drop. Crate marked "TIMBER, JOVIAN STORE, FOR ARMS WORKS". Eight walnut logs, real wood, real
Earth wood, rescued before the end and kept at Jupiter for rifle stocks. Signed for by the quartermaster who
then stood and smelled them for a full minute. So did I. So did the guard.

**Thu.** Cemetery plot extended. They brought up four more sacks of Earth soil from the Spaceport. The only real
dirt on this moon and it's for the dead. Seems right, somehow.

**Fri.** Pirate frigate seen low over the north ridge. Didn't stop. Took the Hut 7 water tank on the way past
with a grapple. Who steals a water tank?

**Sat.** Everyone, apparently. Hut 7 says they'd pay for one.

**Sun.** Rest day. Nobody rests.
