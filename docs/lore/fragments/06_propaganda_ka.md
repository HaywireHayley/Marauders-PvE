**KINGDOM ALLIANCE — MINISTRY OF INFORMATION — LEAFLET No. 19**

# CARELESS LAMPS COST SHIPS

You may think the pirate is a ruffian with a cutlass. He is not.
He reads cargo manifests. He listens to the radio. He drinks in your bar.

**Do not** discuss sailing times, cargoes or gate schedules in public places.
**Do not** leave portholes unshuttered during transit.
**Do not** accept work, drink or a lift from persons unknown to you.

Should you observe anything untoward, mention it quietly to any officer.
The Kingdom is most grateful for your discretion.

*Lux in tenebris.*
