**REPLY, OVERLORD HOUSE, CLERK'S OFFICE (stamped)**

Mr Wolff,

The House notes your new schedule with interest. The House notes that your gunpowder price has risen 4 marks a
tin since the House arranged your last shipment of saltpetre.

The House would be glad to continue arranging shipments, at a rate to be agreed, plus the customary
consideration, plus interest on the customary consideration.

The House values your friendship. Interest on friendship accrues monthly.

*For Overlord: Clerk 9.*
