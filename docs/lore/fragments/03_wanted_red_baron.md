# WANTED
## THE PIRATE KNOWN AS "THE RED BARON"

**FOR:** piracy on the gate lanes; theft of Allied stores; the murder of Allied servicemen; the unlawful
removal of colonists; arson; impersonation of an officer.

**DESCRIPTION:** Male. Height variously reported as 5 ft 8 in and 6 ft 3 in. Wears a long coat and a peaked
cap with a red band. Speaks with an accent (German; Polish; Irish; reports differ). Is not known to remove
the cap.

**SHIP:** A frigate painted red at the bow. Has been seen with a red stern, also with no red at all.

**REWARD:** 40,000 Allied scrip for information leading to his capture, payable on positive identification.

**NOTE:** Three men have so far been brought in as the Baron. None was. Persons presenting a cap alone will
**not** be paid.

*By order of the Provost Marshal, United Allies Fleet Command, Sector 9.*
