# THE FREE VOICE
### No. 37 — Printed on a press we took — Pass it on, then burn it

**THREE EMPIRES, ONE BOOT.**
The Central Empire works you. The Allies feed you on promises. The Kingdom sells you and calls it trade. In 1951
ordinary people stood up and said **enough**, and the kings and generals had to listen. We are their children.

**NEWS FROM THE ROCKS.** Comrades at the Iridium mine report a gallery gone warm with A742 and the Company still
cutting. Miners: walk out. The Company cannot dig with its own hands.

**TO OUR FRIENDS IN BETA 5.** Hold on. We have not forgotten you.

**A WORD ON PIRATES.** Some comrades ask why we trade with marauders. We trade with them because they bring
rifles and the empires bring chains. Do not mistake them for friends. A marauder will sell you your own
liberation and charge you for the ride.

**WANTED BY THE BASE:** a doctor; a welder; anybody who knows how a scrubber works.

**ARMOURY:** 1,412 rounds, all calibres. *Count them before you take one.*
