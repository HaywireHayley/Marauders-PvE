**WARDROOM — DINNER — SATURDAY**
*Guest night. Mess dress. The Band of the Fleet will play.*

**Clear soup** (*algae, Lab 4*)
**Fillet of fish** (*tinned, Sector 9*)
**Roast fowl** (*one fowl, carved thin, by the Captain's grace*)
**Potatoes** (*real, from the Terraformer allocation — guests are asked not to mention this to the ratings*)
**Pudding** (*ask the steward*)
**Port** (*passed to the left*)

**THE LOYAL TOAST** will be taken seated.

**ABSENT FRIENDS.** By regulation, places are laid for officers of this ship lost in the last thirty days. Tonight
the steward will lay four. Officers will not sit in those places, move the glasses, or eat the bread.

**NOTICE FROM THE COMMANDER:** following the incident on the boat deck, officers will carry their sidearms to
dinner. The silver has been replaced with tin. It is not to be remarked upon.

*(on the back, in pencil, a midshipman:)* *Six by the end of the month, I reckon. Bet you a potato.*
