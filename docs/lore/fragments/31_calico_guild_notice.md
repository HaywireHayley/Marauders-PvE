**THE MARAUDERS — GUILD NOTICE**
*Posted on the counter. Don't lean on it.*

**DUES.** Dues are going up. You knew that. Pay at the counter. I take scrip, marks, crowns, iridium and
gunpowder at my rates, which are fair, because I set them.

**SHARES OF THE DEAD.** If you've left a share with me for someone, they get it. If they don't come for it in a
year, the guild drinks to you with it. That's the rule and it's a good one.

**THE BARON'S CAP.** I have three. They are all genuine. I'm not buying a fourth.

**RECRUITS.** New hands sign the book. If you can't write, make a mark. If you can't make a mark, you're no use
on a torch.

**GOOD CONDUCT.** No fighting at the counter. No opening lead cans in the shop. Last fellow who did it is now
a smudge on the ceiling and his share's still unpaid.

**WHY I'VE ONE EYE.** Because I cleaned my pistol drunk. That's the whole story. Stop asking.

— *Calico, First Mate, keeper of the book*
