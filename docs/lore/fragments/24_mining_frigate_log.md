**SHIP'S LOG — MINING FRIGATE "GUTBUCKET" (Reg. CE-M-0471) — Master: J. Halloran**

**Day 88.** Grappled rock K-19. Good iron, some nickel. Crusher running sweet.

**Day 89.** Rock K-19 not as good as we thought. Crusher running. Crew complaining about the noise. Crew
always complains about the noise. Crew complains when it stops.

**Day 91.** Escort frigate recalled by Company. Company says the lane is "quiet". Company lives on a station.

**Day 93.** Picked up a frigate on the long scope, no lights, holding off at 40 miles. Not hailing. Doubled the
watch on the hold doors. Issued the trench guns. Half the men have never fired one.

**Day 94.** Still there. Moved closer in the night. Ordered the crusher kept running so they'd think we hadn't
noticed. Ordered the strongroom key moved from my cabin to the cook.

**Day 95.** Company replies: "Continue operations. Pirate activity not expected in your lane."

**Day 95, later.** Grapples fouled. Something cut the line. Lights on the scope now, four of them, and they're
not in a hurry.

*(The rest of the page is blank. The next entry is in another hand:)* *Thanks for the iron. — the Dark*
