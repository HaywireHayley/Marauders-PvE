**UA COLONY CRUISER PROVIDENCE — NOTICE No. 212**
**ISSUED BY: Purser's Office, Deck 4. Day 261 of transit.**

Effective 0600 tomorrow, rations for awake personnel are revised as follows:

| Item | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Fungus loaf | 3 slices | 2 slices |
| Protein paste | 1 tube | 1 tube (unchanged!) |
| Coffee substitute | 2 cups | 1 cup |
| Sugar | 2 lumps | Sundays |
| Water | 3 pints | 3 pints, **no washing of clothes** |

**Reason:** Lab 2 culture lost to contamination. Lab 1 working double shifts. The Purser thanks the science
staff for their efforts and asks all hands **not** to visit the labs to "help".

**Hibernation reserves:** Pods 300-340 will be woken on Day 270 to replace losses. Personnel in those pods
will be issued half rations for the first week, as they will not notice.

*Hoarding is an offence under the Articles of Transit.*

*Keep your chin up — we're over two-thirds of the way there!*
