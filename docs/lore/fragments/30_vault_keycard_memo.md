**MEMORANDUM — ALL KEYCARD HOLDERS — REVISED PROCEDURE**
From: Station Security Office. Copy to: Quartermaster, Paymaster, Chaplain (*why is the Chaplain on this*).

Following the losses of last quarter, the following is effective immediately.

1. Keycards are **not** to be worn on a string around the neck. Pirates have learned this.
2. Keycards are **not** to be kept in the top drawer of the desk. Pirates have learned this also.
3. Keycards are **not** to be hidden inside a Bible. See 2.
4. Keycards are to be kept on the person, in the inside breast pocket, buttoned.
5. Should a holder be killed, the card is considered compromised. The vault lock is to be changed at once.
   Lock changes take nine days. During those nine days, see 6.
6. During lock changes the vault is guarded by two men. If the two men are killed, see 5.

Holders are reminded that a blowtorch will open the vault anyway. The keycard exists so that **we** do not
need a blowtorch.

*Initial and return.*
