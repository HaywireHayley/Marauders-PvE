`[POD RECORDER // AUTO-START ON LAUNCH // WIRE TAPE]`

Pod six. Launched 0217, ship's time. It's me, Cardew, steward. There's three of us. Me, the cook, and the
purser's boy, Abe, he's eleven.

Air gauge says thirty-six hours. Beacon lamp's on. Abe is pressing the button. I've told him it only needs
pressing once.

We saw the ship go. Not all at once. The lights went section by section, like someone walking through turning
them off.

Cook brought the cash box. I asked him why. He said, "In case anyone picks us up who'd want paying." He's not
wrong.

Hour nine. Something on the scope. Abe's waving at the port. They're not navy. They've got a torch on the
front and no paint on the bow.

Cook's opening the cash box.

If this gets heard by anyone: the *Hesper*, Sector 9, all hands but six. And Abe pressed the button.

`[TAPE CONTINUES, SOUND OF A HULL BEING CUT. RECORDING ENDS]`
