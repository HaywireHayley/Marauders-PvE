*(taped above the throttle of a frigate, fingerprinted with grease)*

**TICK'S RULES. DON'T TOUCH THE TAPE.**

1. Never say "last run". Say "this run". Last runs come true.
2. Tap the JTA plate twice on the way out. Once is rude, three's greedy.
3. Never whistle in the throat of the gate. The gate whistles back.
4. Count your crew out loud before you go through. Count them again after. If it's different, don't
   count a third time.
5. Clocks lose a minute in the gate. Don't wind them back. That minute's paying for something.
6. A lead can goes through in the hold, never in your lap.
7. Don't look out the port during transit. You'll see the black. The black sees you back.
8. If the gate starts closing, go. Don't wait for anyone. They'd not wait for you. (They would. Go anyway.)
9. Nobody ever came back through a closed gate. Nobody's ever come back to complain, either.

*— T.D., 212 transits, all of them this run*
