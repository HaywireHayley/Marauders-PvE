Dear Marta,

They let us write once a quarter now so I am making it long. I am well. The food is [CENSORED] but there
is a lot of it on Sundays. I work in the smelter on the second shift. My hands are fine, the burns are mostly
on the left.

My release date is still the 4th of May 1987. The clerk writes it in pencil, which he says is
for neatness.

There was a [CENSORED] in Block C in the spring. You may have heard. I was not involved. Warden Mott made
a speech after and said that any man who keeps his head down will see his family again. I am keeping my head
so far down I can see my boots.

Some of the men talk about the pirates coming. They say a pirate ship took forty men off Beta 3 last year.
I asked what happened to the forty. Nobody knows. I would rather do my time.

Tell Pavel to stay in school, and to stay off the gates. Tell him his father is a respectable smelter.

Your loving husband,
J.

*[Passed. 3 lines withheld. Prison Security, Censor's Office, stamp 14.]*
