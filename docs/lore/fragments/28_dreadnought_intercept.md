`[GUNNERY NET // ENCRYPTED, BADLY // HISS, CLICK OF A HAND CRANK]`

**TURRET B:** Fire control, B turret. Electric hoist's down again. Going to hand.

**FIRE CONTROL:** B turret, how long to load on hand?

**TURRET B:** Ninety seconds a round if Kowalski doesn't faint. Sixty if Kowalski faints, he's slower than
the replacement.

**FIRE CONTROL:** Noted. Captain wants a full broadside on the scope contact at twenty-three thousand.

**TURRET B:** The scope contact is a frigate, sir. A small one.

**FIRE CONTROL:** Captain is aware.

**TURRET B:** It's a lot of shell for a small frigate, sir.

**FIRE CONTROL:** The Captain's last small frigate came aboard through the boat deck and took the wardroom
silver. Load and report.

**TURRET B:** Loading. *(off mike)* Crank it, lads. For the silver.

`[SOUND: DISTANT, STEADY CRANKING]`

**FIRE CONTROL:** All turrets. Contact has vanished from scope. Repeat, vanished. Check your own hatches.

`[CARRIER LOST]`
