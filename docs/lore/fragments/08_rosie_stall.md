**ROSIE'S — Wants & Whispers**

*Today I'm after:*
- Medical kits, any make, even Allied. Especially Allied.
- Clean bandage, not "clean" bandage.
- Fungus spawn from a cruiser lab, sealed jar. Two jars and I'll kiss you. Three and I'll pay.
- Radio valves. I don't care what they came out of.

*Today I'll tell you, for the price of a cuppa:*

- The 47th's cruiser on the long haul woke its reserve pods early. That means they're short on hands, or short
  on air. Either way they're short.
- Navy Outpost changed its duty slate on Monday and nobody's fixed the night watch yet.
- Wolff bought every Thompson drum on the station. Somebody's planning a party.
- Calico says the guild's dues are going up. Calico always says that.

*I don't do credit, I don't do sob stories, and I don't do goats. Greybeard sent you, didn't he.*

— R.
