`[CHANNEL 4 // GATE 7 TRAFFIC // HISS]`

**GATE CONTROL:** Unidentified frigate on bearing two-one-zero, you are inside the approach lane. Transmit your
transit chit or heave to for inspection.

**FRIGATE:** Gate Seven, this is the *Patience of Saints*, colonial hauler, fifty tons of seed potatoes and a sick
goat. Chit's in the post.

**GATE CONTROL:** *Patience*, you are showing four gun ports and no cargo booms.

**FRIGATE:** Very big goat.

**GATE CONTROL:** Heave to. Mine field is live on your current heading. Repeat, live.

**FRIGATE:** Copy, live mines, thank you kindly. Lads, pull the starboard sweep in, we'll take the low road.

**GATE CONTROL:** *Patience*, there is no low road. *Patience*, respond.

`[STATIC, 11 SECONDS]`

**GATE CONTROL:** Log it. Unidentified vessel transited at 0412 without chit. Second time this week. Somebody
tell the Navy we need that picket ship back.

**UNKNOWN VOICE, FAINT:** Navy's busy, love.

`[CARRIER LOST]`
