**SPACEPORT AUTHORITY: NOTICE TO ARRIVING PASSENGERS**
**Customs Hall B. By order. Rev. 6 (1984).**

1. Have your transit chit, ration card and inoculation stub IN HAND. Officers will not wait while you search.
2. The following must be declared: firearms, ammunition, powders of any grade, fuses, radio valves, medical
   alcohol, seed stock, livestock, and any substance in a lead container.
3. Lead containers will be opened in the shielded booth only. Do not open them in the queue. *This means you.*
4. Undeclared goods are forfeit. Forfeit goods are not returned. Do not ask Desk 3.
5. Passengers found with stamped Navy property will be detained pending enquiry.
6. Luggage unclaimed after 14 days is auctioned. Urns are held for 90 days.
7. Spitting, gambling and the singing of political songs are prohibited in the hall.

The bar on Concourse C is open to ticketed passengers only.

The Authority accepts no liability for losses arising from piracy, decompression, or acts of the empires.

*Thank you for travelling.*
