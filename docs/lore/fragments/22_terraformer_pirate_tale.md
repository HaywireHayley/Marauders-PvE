`[REEL-TO-REEL, "TAPE 9 — DON'T RECORD OVER", MUSIC AND GLASSES IN BACKGROUND]`

**OLD VOICE:** ...no, no, listen. I was there. I was on the second boat. Proxima, '76.

**YOUNG VOICE:** You were never.

**OLD VOICE:** Second boat. We did the grain. The Baron did the men. Didn't like it, the Baron didn't. Said it
was business. Said someone'd paid for farmers, not for grain, and the grain was our bonus.

**YOUNG VOICE:** Who paid?

**OLD VOICE:** Ha. Who paid. Fella in a good coat came aboard after. Didn't give a name. Had a cog on his
cufflinks. Draw your own conclusions.

**YOUNG VOICE:** And the flash? They say he nuked the place.

**OLD VOICE:** *(long pause)* We were through the gate before. I saw a light in the port. Could've been the
platform. Could've been the gate. I tell you what, the Baron never talked about it, not once, not drunk, and
he talked about everything drunk.

**YOUNG VOICE:** So he did it.

**OLD VOICE:** I said I was on the second boat, son. Buy me another and I'll remember what I didn't see.

`[LAUGHTER. TAPE RUNS OUT]`
