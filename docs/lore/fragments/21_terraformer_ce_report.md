**CENTRAL EMPIRE — AGRICULTURAL DIRECTORATE — MEMORANDUM 76/1193**
**CLASSIFICATION: INTERNAL. DESTROY AFTER READING.** *(not destroyed)*

To: Inspector K. Volkova
Re: Allied allegations concerning the Proxima platform

1. The Directorate has no knowledge of the incident referred to by the Allied press.
2. The Directorate notes that Platform Kursk-2 has received an unexpected allocation of 12 skilled
   agricultural labourers and 340 tons of seed grain of Allied type. These are to be logged as "recovered
   salvage, origin unknown".
3. No payment has been made to any irregular party. The 18,000 marks entered under "Transport, misc." are a
   clerical matter.
4. Labourers are to be accommodated to the standard of Category C and are not to be interviewed by any person
   from outside the Directorate.
5. In the event of further Allied enquiry, the Directorate's position is that the platform was lost to an
   industrial accident, regrettable and in no way connected to the Empire.

It is so ordered.

*(signed, illegible)*
