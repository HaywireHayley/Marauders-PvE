**STANDING ORDERS — GARRISON — "THE FORTRESS"**
*By authority of Major R. Ffoulkes, SAS. Supersedes all previous. Read and initial.*

1. The Fortress does not exist. You are not stationed at it. Your letters will say "Sector Depot".
2. The vault level is entered by two men, never one. Each holds half the keycard set. Neither is told the
   other's half.
3. Visitors without a Ministry chit are to be detained, offered tea, and not released.
4. Pirates, by contrast, are to be shot. **Unless** they present the phrase of the week, in which case they
   are contractors and are to be escorted to the Major. The phrase this week is in the sealed envelope.
   Do not open the envelope to check.
5. Operatives returning from the field are not to be asked where they have been.
6. The chapel memorial carries spaces for future names. These are not to be used for jokes.
7. Tea at 1600 is not optional. It is the only fixed point in the Kingdom.
