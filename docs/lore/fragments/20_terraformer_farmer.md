*(written years later, in a careful hand, on CE work-camp paper)*

To my daughter, if this reaches you.

You asked me what happened at the Proxima platform. You were four. You remember the wheat smell, I think, and
not the rest.

It was harvest week in '76. Dome 3 was gold to the glass. The alarm went at second bell. We thought it was
another seal blowing. Then the lights went and men came in through the seed vault, masks on, one in a long
coat who did not shout. They took the grain first. Then they took the men who knew the grain.

I was counted with the seed drills. Twelve of us went in a frigate's hold, in the dark, to a CE platform where
the soil was worse and the guards were polite. I have grown their wheat ever since.

I do not know if the platform was burned after. They say there was a flash. They say a lot of things.

I do not know who paid the man in the coat. I know the wheat they make me grow is ours. I'd know it anywhere.

Your father, Tomas
