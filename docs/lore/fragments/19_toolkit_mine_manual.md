**STANDARD FIELD TOOLKIT — USER'S MANUAL — p. 22**
**SECTION 6: CORRIDOR MINES (PRESSURE AND TRIPWIRE PATTERNS)**

*Contents of kit used: wire snips (B), pliers long-nose (C), torch pocket (F), safety pins (K), chalk (M).*

**6.1** Do not run. Mines are laid where men run.

**6.2** Look at the floor, then the walls at ankle height, then the floor again.

**6.3** A pressure mine shows a domed lid, usually painted to match the deck. Insert safety pin (K) into the
hole on the side **before** touching the lid. If there is no hole, there is no pin. Mark it and go round.

**6.4** A tripwire mine: follow the wire to **both** ends before cutting. If both ends are fixed to mines,
consult 6.7.

**6.5** Never cut a wire that is taut. Never cut a wire that is slack. Cut only the wire you have followed.

**6.6** Chalk an X on every mine you have made safe. Chalk a circle on every mine you have not. Leave the
circles to the sappers.

**6.7** See 6.1.

---

**SPACE MINES** (small and nuclear pattern) are not to be approached with this kit under any circumstances.
They are dealt with by the ship's guns, from far away, by somebody else.
