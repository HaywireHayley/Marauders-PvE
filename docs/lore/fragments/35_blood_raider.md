*(a brown paper tag tied to the collar with wire)*

**ITEM: Raider suit, worn. Size: will fit.**
**CONDITION: Stained. Will not wash out. Has been tried.**

Came off the *Red Hand*'s boarding chief after the Penal Colony job in '81. He went in first through the riot
doors with a torch and a trench gun and came out last, and none of what's on the suit is his. Wore it to the bar
that night. Wore it to every job after. Said the stains kept the guards honest: they'd see him coming down a
corridor and drop their rifles before he'd said a word.

It worked until it didn't. Suit came back on a pod without him.

Crews call it the Blood Raider now and won't wear it aboard, mostly. Some do. They say it remembers. I say it
smells.

**PRICE:** Fair. Not cheap. You're paying for the reputation, and the reputation's earned.

— *tagged by the Pirate Trader, stall 4*
