# Loading lines

One-line hints for loading screens or the minimap's text panel. Each is under 120 characters. Tag format:
`[place or faction]`; `(FT)` marks a line that leans on fan theory (A742, the 1976 raid, the 1951 rebels), in case the
maintainers want to keep those out of always-on screens.

## General / the world
1. [World] Seventy years in the shadow of the Great War. Earth is a rumour now. The air you breathe is metered.
2. [World] Three empires carved up the dark. None of them can police it. That's where you come in.
3. [World] Nobody owns the gates. Everybody charges for them.
4. [World] Earth soil sells by the sack. Most of it goes to cemeteries.
5. [World] Paper forms come in triplicate. One for the empire, one for the clerk, one for the pirate who steals the desk.
6. [World] Every frigate has a dead man's name under the paint.
7. [World] Lead canisters stay shut. Ask anyone. Ask the ceiling of Calico's shop.

## Factions
8. [Central Empire] The Directorate does not lose platforms. It records industrial accidents.
9. [Central Empire] CE posters say output is 112% of plan. CE miners say the posters are 100% of the output.
10. [Central Empire] A CE officer never apologises. He issues a correction.
11. [United Allies] UA Marines are promised three squares a day. Ask them how many they get.
12. [United Allies] The 47th Marines' bulldog is on every UA locker. So is a picture of somebody's mum.
13. [United Allies] UA Medics are worth stopping first. A Marine with a medic behind him gets up again.
14. [Kingdom Alliance] The Kingdom never admits to an operation. It just pays someone else to have one.
15. [Kingdom Alliance] If an SAS man offers you tea, he is not being friendly. Drink it anyway.
16. [Kingdom Alliance] The Ministry pays bounties on the Red Baron. Rumour says it pays the Baron, too.
17. [Rebels] (FT) The Rebels say their grandparents shot the generals in 1951. They still can't afford the bullets.
18. [Rebels] Rebels trade with marauders. They don't trust them. Nobody's asking them to.
19. [Marauders] Loyal to no king or country. Loyal to the crew, until the gate starts closing.
20. [Marauders] The torch goes first. The torch is paid extra.
21. [Marauders] Leave the farmers be. Farmers grow what we eat. Their stores are fair game.

## Places
22. [Spaceport] Customs Hall B confiscates undeclared goods. Desk 3 sells them back on Thursdays.
23. [Spaceport] The lost-luggage office keeps urns for ninety days. Most are never claimed.
24. [Navy Outpost] The duty slate is chalk. Names get wiped too often to bother with ink.
25. [Navy Outpost] The east approach was re-sown with mines on Tuesday. Nobody walks the east approach.
26. [Asteroid Mine] Iridium miners pay rent on their own pressure suits.
27. [Asteroid Mine] (FT) When the rock face goes warm and the compass drifts, it isn't iridium any more.
28. [Mini Mine] Days without accident: 0. The board has been repainted four times.
29. [Mini Mine] The guard at the shaft head is there for the ore, not the miners.
30. [Mining Frigate] The crusher's emergency stop is padlocked. The foreman has the key.
31. [Mining Frigate] Mining companies recall their escorts when the lane is "quiet". It's quiet because you're in it.
32. [Penal Colony] Release dates at Beta 5 are written in pencil.
33. [Penal Colony] Prisoners smelt metal for the empire that jailed them. The smelter never closes.
34. [Penal Colony] Pirates who take prisoners off a penal colony aren't freeing them. They're selling them a ride.
35. [Rebel Base] The Rebel armoury counts its bullets one at a time. So should you.
36. [Rebel Base] The Free Voice is printed on a stolen press. Pass it on, then burn it.
37. [Fortress] The Fortress does not exist. Its garrison writes home from "Sector Depot".
38. [Fortress] The chapel plaque has blank lines riveted on already. They expect to fill them.
39. [Merchant Ship] Every merchant runs two manifests. The true one is in the strongroom.
40. [Merchant Ship] The captain will give you Hold 1 and look tearful. Go to the strongroom.
41. [Colony Cruiser] Half the colonists are asleep. The reserve pods wake in order of usefulness.
42. [Colony Cruiser] Fungus loaf, two slices. Sugar on Sundays. No washing of clothes.
43. [Terraformer] Farmers on a terraformer are listed on the manifest as agricultural equipment.
44. [Terraformer] (FT) Proxima, 1976: the grain was taken, then the farmers. Nobody agrees about the flash.
45. [Planet Killer] Two keys, two officers, forty turns of the crank. The crew sleep inside the blast door.
46. [Planet Killer] (FT) The warheads are A742. The card on the wall says: remember Dudinka.
47. [Moon Base] The only real soil on the moon is in the cemetery.
48. [Moon Base] Rifle stocks are carved from Earth walnut, stored on Jupiter. Quartermasters stop to smell it.
49. [Capital Ship] The officers' mess lays places for the dead of the last thirty days. Nobody touches the bread.
50. [Dreadnought] The shell hoists have a hand-cranked backup. The backup is used daily.
51. [Space gates] Never say "last run". Say "this run".
52. [Space gates] Tap the gate plate twice on the way out. Once is rude, three's greedy.
53. [Space gates] The gate closes for no one. Nobody's ever come back through to complain.
54. [Escape pods] Escape pods keep a recorder running. Listen before you open the hatch.
55. [Vaults] Keycards hang on strings round guards' necks. The guards have been told to stop that.
56. [Mines] Pressure mines are painted to match the deck. Look down, then at ankle height, then down again.

## People
57. [Red Baron] Three men have been brought in as the Red Baron. None of them was.
58. [Red Baron] The Baron never takes off the cap. Some say there's nobody under it.
59. [Traders] Greybeard was on the last shuttle from Earth. The year changes every time he tells it.
60. [Traders] Overlord charges interest on everything, friendship included. It accrues monthly.
